USB Boot Keyboard

Turn an ESP32-S3 into a USB keyboard that Home Assistant can type on — built as a single-interface boot keyboard, the one shape cheap KVM hotkey sniffers actually recognise.

Your browser can't flash devices. Use desktop Chrome or Edge — Web Serial isn't available in Safari, Firefox, or on mobile. This page has to be served over HTTPS to flash a device.

Before you plug it in

The USB port becomes the keyboard, so the board does not offer a serial console once the firmware runs. To flash it you must enter the ROM bootloader by hand:

  1. Unplug the board.
  2. Hold the BOOT button down.
  3. Plug the USB cable in, then release BOOT.
  4. Press Connect above and pick the serial port that appears.

After flashing

  1. The board starts a Wi-Fi access point. Join it and the captive portal will ask for your network.
  2. It then appears in Home Assistant under Settings → Devices & services as an ESPHome device.
  3. Later updates go over the air from ESPHome — no BOOT button, no cable.

Improv over serial is not offered on purpose: the serial port is the keyboard, so Wi-Fi setup has to go through the captive portal.
